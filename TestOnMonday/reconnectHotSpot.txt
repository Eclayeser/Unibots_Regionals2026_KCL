Method 1: The Absolute Quickest Way (If Flashing a New SD Card)
If you haven't set up your SD card yet, you can pre-configure the Wi-Fi credentials before the Pi even boots for the first time.

    Turn on your laptop's mobile hotspot so you know the exact network name (SSID) and password.

    Open the Raspberry Pi Imager on your laptop.

    Choose your Pi device (Raspberry Pi 5), your OS (Raspberry Pi OS 64-bit), and your storage (the SD card).

    Click Next. A prompt will appear asking if you want to apply OS Customisation settings. Click Edit Settings.

    Under the General tab:

        Check the box for Set hostname (e.g., robotpi).

        Check the box for Configure wireless LAN.

        Enter your laptop hotspot's SSID and Password.

        Important: Make sure the "Wireless LAN country" is correct.

    Under the Services tab:

        Enable SSH (use password authentication or public key) so you can control the Pi from your laptop.

    Save and write the image. When you put the SD card in the Pi 5 and power it on, it will automatically connect to your hotspot.




Method 2: Headless Setup (If the SD card is already flashed)
If your SD card already has the OS on it and you don't have a monitor or keyboard to plug into the Pi, you can force the OS to connect by adding a file to the boot partition.

Note: Even though Bookworm uses NetworkManager, Raspberry Pi OS includes a script that will automatically convert old-style wpa_supplicant files into NetworkManager profiles on boot.

    Remove the SD card from the Pi and plug it into your laptop.

    Open the SD card's bootfs (or just boot) drive in your file explorer.

    Create a new text file in the root of this drive and name it exactly wpa_supplicant.conf. (Make sure your laptop doesn't secretly save it as wpa_supplicant.conf.txt).

    Open the file in a text editor and paste the following, replacing the network name and password with your laptop's hotspot details:


ctrl_interface=DIR=/var/run/wpa_supplicant GROUP=netdev
update_config=1
country=GB 

network={
    ssid="YOUR_HOTSPOT_NAME"
    psk="YOUR_HOTSPOT_PASSWORD"
    key_mgmt=WPA-PSK
}

    Eject the SD card, put it in the Pi, and power it on. It should connect to your hotspot within a minute or two.
